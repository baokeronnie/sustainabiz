About SustainaBiz

Better business for a better people and planet.

We are a Botswana-based business consulting firm focused on ESG and sustainability management. Our work helps organisations preserve long-term value while creating positive social, environmental and economic outcomes.

Every organisation has a different mission, operating context and community. That is why our services are curated, evidence-led and designed to move clients from awareness to implementation.
